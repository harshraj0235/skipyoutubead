💬 Open a Support Ticket / Issue

❓ How do I report an ad that wasn't blocked?

YouTube regularly experiments with new player layouts. If you encounter a new ad format, open an issue on our GitHub repository with the video URL and your browser version so we can update detection rules immediately.

❓ How does the Movie Music Skipper work?

The extension monitors YouTube's auto-generated subtitle cues for musical symbols (such as ♪, [Music], [Instrumental]) and prolonged pauses in dialogue. When detected in movies, it automatically advances 6 minutes so you can enjoy uninterrupted storytelling.

❓ Can I change the sensitivity or skip duration?

Yes! Simply click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the settings popup. You can select between 3 operating modes (Full Protection, Ad Blocker Only, or Music Skipper Only) and adjust caption sensitivity between Low, Medium, and High.

❓ Is any of my personal data tracked?

No. The extension operates completely locally in your browser memory and collects zero personal data, zero web history, and zero cookies. Read our full Privacy Policy for complete details.
