Privacy Policy for Microsoft Edge

Ad Blocker for YouTube™ — Skip Ads & Music Skipper
Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Developer Policies for Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store Compliant

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Guarantee

This extension runs 100% locally on your computer inside Microsoft Edge. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information, browsing history, or identifiers. All operations occur strictly on-device in browser memory.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with Developer Policies for the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store, we disclose that this extension collects zero user data:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (name, email address, IP address, device telemetry)
  • No Browsing History or Search Queries
  • No YouTube Account Information or Passwords
  • No Keystroke Logging or User Activity Tracking
  • No Analytics, Telemetry, or External Tracking Pixels
  • No Cookies Stored, Read, or Transmitted Off the Device

2. Local Storage Usage (chrome.storage)

The extension utilizes the standard browser chrome.storage API solely to store user preferences locally on the device:

  • Selected Operation Mode (Full Protection, Ad Blocker Only, or Music Skipper Only)
  • Music detection sensitivity setting (Low, Medium, or High)
  • Notification sound toggle preferences
  • Local skip statistics (number of ads skipped and estimated time saved)

This data is never sent to external servers and can be wiped instantly by uninstalling the extension from Microsoft Edge.

3. Permissions Justification

This extension adheres strictly to the Principle of Least Privilege in the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store:

  • storage — Required to save user preferences locally across browser restarts.
  • https://www.youtube.com/* (Host Permission) — Required to inject content scripts into YouTube video watch pages to detect ad elements, click native skip buttons, read subtitle markers for music skipping, and display in-player control buttons.

No permissions are requested for any other website, tab, or background process.

4. Zero Remote Code Declaration

All JavaScript, CSS, HTML, and icon files are bundled directly inside the extension archive. The extension contains zero eval() calls, no external script tags, and loads no remote resources or WebAssembly from third-party CDNs.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

The extension contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data brokers. Because no data is collected, no data is sold, rented, or transferred to third parties under any circumstances.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The extension is family-friendly and safe for users of all ages. It does not collect any information from children under the age of 13.
