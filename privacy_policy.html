Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Ad Blocker for YouTube™ — Skip Ads & Music Skipper ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles information.

Summary: This Extension does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information. All data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Extension operates entirely on your local device. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected
  • No browsing history is collected or tracked
  • No user identifiers are created or stored
  • No analytics or telemetry data is gathered
  • No data is transmitted to any external servers
  • No cookies are created or read

2. Local Storage

The Extension uses Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync API solely to store your user preferences (such as skip mode selection and toggle states). This data:

  • Is stored locally on your device via Chrome's secure storage mechanism
  • Syncs across your Chrome browsers only if you are signed into Chrome (a built-in Chrome feature)
  • Contains only extension settings (e.g., "skip mode: both", "music sensitivity: medium")
  • Does not contain any personally identifiable information
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the Extension

3. Permissions Justification

The Extension requests the following permissions, each with a specific purpose:

  • storage — To save and retrieve your Extension settings and preferences locally.
  • host_permissions (youtube.com) — To run the content script on YouTube pages for video navigation functionality. The Extension only operates on youtube.com and no other websites.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to any third parties. There is no user data to share.

6. Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The Extension does not collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out by creating an issue on the Extension's support page.
